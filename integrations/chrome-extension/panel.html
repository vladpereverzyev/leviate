Webcam

Scan with your phone

Your phone becomes the camera of Leviate. Connect the phone and this computer to the same Wi‑Fi network.

To find each other, the phone and this computer go through the free PeerJS server (0.peerjs.com) and a STUN server of Google, which see the IP addresses of both devices but never the video. The video goes straight from the phone to this computer, encrypted and never recorded.

Gestures

Open hand One finger Two fingers

Open hand moves the blue cursor on the page. Hold one finger still for a left click and keep holding for a double click, two fingers for a right click.

Any other pose, a fist for example, leaves the cursor where it is.
