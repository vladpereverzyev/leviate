# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Leviate to be a friendly place for everyone who uses it or helps build it,
whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, religion or sexual
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, personal attacks or derogatory comments.
- Sexual language or imagery and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing private information of others without their permission.
- Sharing patient data or any scan that can identify a person.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer
[@vladpereverzyev](https://github.com/vladpereverzyev) on GitHub or use the
"Report content" option that GitHub offers on every comment. Reports are handled
privately and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response that fits the
situation. It can be a private warning, a public warning, the removal of content,
a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project.
